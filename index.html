പത്രം പഠിക്കാം Pathram Padikkam: Learn Newspaper English

A skill, not the news: learn how headline English works, then practise on headlines picked because they teach something. വാർത്ത അറിയാനല്ല, ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് പത്രം വായിക്കാൻ പഠിക്കാൻ.

Personal, non-commercial study project for learning to read English newspapers. It is not a news source: headlines are kept only because of how they are written, not for their news.

Headlines quoted for study are from newspapers such as The Hindu and remain © their publishers. Only the headline text is quoted; no article text, links or images are kept. Not affiliated with any newspaper. The rules, word lists and Malayalam meanings are study aids written for this page.
